TimePrice keeps wage, wishlist, focus sessions, unlock grants, and the deep-work log on the device. There is no account, no server, and no analytics.

The Safari extension reads a small wage snapshot from the App Group so it can label prices. It does not send page contents anywhere. Screen Time choices stay in the App Group and are used only to shield and unshield apps on that iPhone.

A CSV time log is imported from a file you pick or from text you paste. TimePrice does not connect to Toggl or any other time tracker.
